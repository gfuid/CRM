Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026 · Last updated: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Travel-Trade ("Travel-Trade", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use the Travel-Trade CRM website at traveltrade.co.in and the Travel-Trade CRM web application (together, the "Service").

The Service is a customer relationship management (CRM) tool for export, import and trade businesses. It lets a business ("Customer") manage its leads, follow-ups, tasks and team, and optionally connect its own WhatsApp Business, email, AI-calling and social media accounts.

1. Who is responsible for your data

  • Account data (the people who sign up for and use the Service): Travel-Trade decides how this data is used and is responsible for it.
  • Customer data (the leads, buyers, contacts, messages and files a Customer puts into the Service, or that reach the Customer through accounts it connects): the Customer decides how this data is used. Travel-Trade processes it only on the Customer's behalf and on its instructions, to provide the Service.

If you are a contact of one of our Customers (for example, a buyer who received a WhatsApp message from a business using the Service), please contact that business first. We will help them answer your request.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, company name, country, password (stored only as a secure hash) and role.
  • Staff accounts that a company owner creates for their team.
  • Business data you enter: leads, contact persons, company details, remarks, follow-ups, tasks, quotations, shipping and costing calculations, and uploaded files.
  • Messages you send us for support.

2.2 Information from accounts you connect

Connecting another service is optional and only the company owner can do it. When a Customer connects one, we receive only what is needed to provide that feature:

  • WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta): WhatsApp Business Account ID, phone number ID, display name, an access token, message templates, and the messages, delivery and read statuses exchanged with the Customer's contacts (including the contacts' phone numbers and profile names).
  • Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts (Meta): the Page or account ID, name and profile picture, an access token, and the posts, comments and basic insights for content the Customer publishes or manages through the Service.
  • Email sending: the mail server or email provider settings the Customer enters, and the open and click information for emails sent through the Service.
  • AI calling and AI assistants: call numbers, call status, recordings and transcripts where the Customer turns them on, and the text sent to the AI provider the Customer has chosen.

Access tokens and API keys are stored encrypted. They are never shown back in full in the app.

2.3 Information collected automatically

  • Technical data such as IP address, browser and device type, and pages used, for security, abuse prevention and fixing errors.
  • Data saved in your browser (local storage) to keep you signed in and remember settings. We do not use advertising cookies or sell data to advertisers.

3. How we use information

  • To create and run your account and provide the features you use.
  • To send and receive WhatsApp messages, emails and social media posts that the Customer asks the Service to send, and to show replies and statuses in the CRM.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent fraud and misuse, and fix problems.
  • To send service emails, such as password resets, daily reports and important notices.
  • To answer support requests.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use data received from Meta platforms (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram) for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than providing the features the Customer has turned on, and we do not share it with data brokers.

4. How we share information

We share information only as described here:

  • Service providers who host or run parts of the Service for us under confidentiality obligations: cloud hosting (Render, Vercel), database hosting (MongoDB), and email delivery.
  • Platforms the Customer connects, such as Meta (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram), the Customer's email provider, telephony providers (for example Twilio) and AI providers (for example OpenAI, Google Gemini or ElevenLabs). Data is sent to them only to perform the action the Customer requested. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Within a Customer's company: company owners and the staff they give access to can see that company's data, according to the permissions the owner sets.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, court order or a government authority, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users or the public.
  • Business transfer: if Travel-Trade is involved in a merger or sale, data may move to the new owner, who must follow this policy.

5. Data storage, transfers and security

Data is stored with our cloud providers, whose servers may be outside India. We protect data with encryption in transit (HTTPS), encryption of stored access tokens and API keys, hashed passwords, separation of each company's data, role-based access, and regular backups.

No online service is completely secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal data, we will inform affected Customers and the authorities as required by law.

6. How long we keep data

  • We keep account and Customer data while the account is active.
  • When a Customer disconnects a WhatsApp, Facebook or Instagram account, we delete the stored access token for it straight away.
  • When an account is deleted, we delete its data within 30 days. Copies in backups are removed within a further 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep certain records (for example invoices) for longer.

7. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it (see our Data Deletion page);
  • withdraw a consent you gave, such as disconnecting a connected account;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to.

You can update most account details yourself inside the app. For anything else, email us at the address below. We reply within 30 days.

8. Children

The Service is meant for businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for important changes, tell account owners by email or inside the app.
