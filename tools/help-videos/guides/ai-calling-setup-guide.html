AI Calling — full setup guide

An AI sales agent (ElevenLabs) calls your buyers in any country from your own phone number, in their office hours, and writes the result into the CRM.
For company owners · about 1–2 hours the first time (plus KYC time at the phone company)

You need two accounts

Phone numberIndia: Vobiz (console.vobiz.ai)
Other countries: Twilio, Telnyx or Plivo		The number the buyer sees. It must allow international outbound calls to your buyers’ countries.

WhatsApp-style tags on your leads (e.g. turmeric-buyer) help you choose whom to call. AI Calling must be switched on for your company by the platform admin.

Part A — ElevenLabs: agent and API key

  1. Sign up at elevenlabs.io and choose a plan that includes Agents minutes (the free plan has only a few minutes for testing).
  2. Open Agents (Agents Platform) → New agent → Blank template. Name it, e.g. “Nova sales agent”. You do not need to write the prompt here — the CRM fills it from your script (Part D).
  3. Pick a voice and the main language of your buyers. Save.
  4. Copy the Agent ID (shown on the agent page, starts with agent_).
  5. Click your profile (bottom left) → API keys → Create API key. Give it a name (“CRM”), allow Agents / Conversational AI access, and copy the key (starts with sk_). It is shown only once.

Part B — Phone number

India: Vobiz

  1. Sign up at console.vobiz.ai and finish KYC with your company documents. Calls start only after KYC is approved.
  2. Buy a number (DID) and add balance to the wallet.
  3. Create an outbound SIP trunk. Note the SIP address / domain, and set a username and password for it. Link your number to the trunk.
  4. Ask Vobiz support to switch on international outbound calling for the countries you need (e.g. UAE, Vietnam, Netherlands), and confirm the per-minute price.

Other countries: Twilio (similar for Telnyx / Plivo)

  1. Sign up at twilio.com, upgrade the account, and buy a number in the country you want (some countries ask for an address or documents).
  2. In Voice → Settings → Geo permissions, tick the countries you will call.
  3. Copy the Account SID and Auth token from the Twilio console home page.

Import the number into ElevenLabs

  1. In ElevenLabs → Agents → Phone numbers → Import number.
  2. Vobiz / any SIP: choose From SIP trunk. Label, your number with country code (+91…), outbound address = the Vobiz SIP domain, transport TCP, and the trunk username + password.
    Twilio: choose From Twilio, paste the number, Account SID and Auth token.
  3. Assign your agent to the number and save. Use ElevenLabs’ own “test call” once to check the line.
Buyers in many countries? You can import several numbers (e.g. an Indian number for most buyers and a US number for +1 buyers). The CRM lets you choose a number per country code.

Part C — Connect in the CRM

  1. Open AI Calling in the left menu → Setup.
  2. Paste the ElevenLabs API key and the Agent ID and press Save & read my numbers. The CRM reads your agent and lists the numbers of your ElevenLabs account.

Numbers, calling hours, retries and costs

  • Call from (default): the number used for most buyers. + Add country: another number for a dial code (e.g. 1 → your US number).
  • Call from / Until are in the buyer’s own time. A buyer in Dubai is called in Dubai office hours, a buyer in Vietnam in Vietnam hours. Choose the working days too.
  • Tries if no answer and Try again after (hours); Calls at the same time (start with 2–3).
  • Cost per minute: type the ElevenLabs price and your phone price (per country if you know it). The CRM then shows the cost of every call and campaign.

Part D — The sales script

  1. In 3. Sales script write, in simple words: about your company, products, specs and prices it may say, the goal of the call (e.g. “find out if they buy turmeric, the monthly quantity, and the email for the price list”), the language and the first sentence.
  2. Words like {{buyer_name}}, {{company_name}}, {{country}}, {{product}} are filled for each buyer.
  3. Press Save script to my agent. The CRM writes the full instructions to your ElevenLabs agent, including what to collect: interested yes/no, quantity, email, call-back time, and “do not call”.
  4. Type your own mobile number and press Test call to me. Talk to it like a buyer and listen. Change the script until you like it.

Part E — Run a campaign

  1. Make sure your leads have a phone number with country code and useful tags.
  2. Campaigns → New campaign: a name, an optional start time, and the buyers — by tags, all leads, or pasted numbers. The CRM shows how many buyers and the estimated cost (2 minutes per call).
  3. Press Start calling. The CRM calls in each buyer’s office hours, a few at a time, and tries again later if nobody answers. You can Pause and Resume.

Part F — Results

  • Calls: every call with its result — Interested, Call back, Not interested, Do not call, No answer.
  • Open a call to read the summary, the details collected (quantity, email, call-back time), the full conversation, and to listen to the recording.
  • The result is also written on the lead: a remark, and for interested or call-back buyers a follow-up date, so your team calls them next. Open the lead → AI calls to see them there.
  • Buyers who say “do not call me” are never called again by the CRM.

A typical sales call lasts 1–3 minutes; unanswered calls cost nothing or very little.

Rules and good practice

  • Call business buyers who can expect a call from an exporter. Respect “do not call” requests and the telemarketing rules of the buyer’s country (in India, TRAI rules for commercial calls).
  • Keep the script short and honest. Prices should come from your team by email or WhatsApp after the call.
  • Start with 10–20 buyers, read the results, improve the script, then call more.

If something does not work

ProblemWhat to do
“Invalid API key” when savingCopy the key again; make sure it has Agents / Conversational AI access.
No numbers shown in the CRMThe number is not imported in ElevenLabs → Agents → Phone numbers (Part B).
Calls fail at onceInternational calling not enabled at Vobiz / Twilio for that country, wrong SIP username/password, or no balance.
Calls not startingOutside the buyer’s calling hours or working days you set — the CRM waits and calls in the next window.
Agent says wrong thingsImprove the script, press “Save script to my agent” again, and make a test call.
