Email Marketing — full setup guide

Send campaigns from your own mailbox (Gmail, Outlook, Zoho or your company mail), see who opened and clicked, and keep it all on the lead.
For company owners · about 10 minutes

Before you start

  • The email address you want to send from, and access to its account settings.
  • For Gmail / Google Workspace and Outlook / Microsoft 365: 2-Step Verification switched on (needed to make an app password).
  • Email Marketing switched on for your company (ask the platform admin if the menu shows a lock).
Why your own mailbox? Buyers see your real address and reply straight to you. The CRM never uses your normal password — only a special “app password” that you can delete any time.

Part A — Make an app password

Gmail / Google Workspace

  1. Open myaccount.google.com → Security. Turn on 2-Step Verification if it is off.
  2. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords (or search “App passwords” in the account search box).
  3. Type a name such as “Travel-Trade CRM” and press Create.
  4. Copy the 16-letter password Google shows. Spaces do not matter.

Google Workspace: if “App passwords” is missing, your Workspace admin must allow 2-Step Verification for users.

Outlook / Microsoft 365

  1. Open account.microsoft.com → Security → Advanced security options, turn on two-step verification.
  2. Under App passwords press Create a new app password and copy it.

Microsoft 365 business: your admin may need to allow “Authenticated SMTP” for the mailbox.

Zoho Mail

  1. Zoho Mail → My Account → Security → App passwords → Generate new password, name “CRM”, copy it.

Other company mail (cPanel, GoDaddy, Hostinger…)

Ask your email host for the SMTP server, port (465 or 587) and use the mailbox password. Choose Other / company mail in the CRM and fill these.

Part B — Connect in the CRM

  1. Open Email Marketing in the left menu.
  2. Type your email address. The CRM picks Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo or Zoho by itself.
  3. Paste the app password and press Save & send test email. When you see Connected! and the test email arrives, you are ready.
  4. Optional: under Advanced, set the daily limit. Leave it blank for the safe default (about 450 a day for Gmail). The CRM stops at the limit and continues the next day by itself.
Do not go above your provider’s limit. Gmail allows about 500 emails a day (Workspace about 2,000). Going over can block sending from the mailbox for a day.

Part C — Choose buyers with tags

  • Add tags on the lead form (e.g. turmeric-buyer, dubai) or for a whole file in Bulk Import (Tags column, or one tag for the file).
  • In Leads, the tag filter shows exactly the buyers in a list.
  • Extra emails of a contact (“Add another email”) are included in campaigns.

Part D — Send a campaign

  1. Templates: save emails you send often (subject + body). Words like {{company_name}} and {{contact_name}} are filled for each buyer.
  2. New Campaign: start from a template or write a new email, give it a name.
  3. Choose buyers: by tags, all leads, or paste a list / CSV of email addresses. Buyers who unsubscribed are left out.
  4. Press Test to me to check how it looks, then Send now or schedule it.

Part E — Results

  • The Dashboard shows today’s limit used, and sent, opened, clicked, failed and unsubscribed.
  • Recent activity shows which buyer opened or clicked — call them while they are interested.
  • Each campaign lists every recipient with opened / clicked.
  • Inside each lead, “Marketing emails” shows every campaign email it got and what the buyer did.
  • Every email has an unsubscribe link; the CRM never emails that buyer again.

“Opened” is counted when the buyer’s email app loads pictures; some apps block this, so real opens can be higher.

If something does not work

ProblemWhat to do
Username and password not acceptedUse the app password, not your normal password. Make a new one if unsure.
No “App passwords” optionTurn on 2-Step Verification first; in Workspace / Microsoft 365 ask your admin.
Emails go to spamSend fewer at a time, avoid all-capital subjects and many links, and ask your IT to set SPF / DKIM for your domain.
Campaign shows “waiting for limit”The daily limit is reached; it continues by itself.
