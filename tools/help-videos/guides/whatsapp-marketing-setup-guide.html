WhatsApp Marketing — full setup guide

Connect your own WhatsApp Business number to the CRM, get templates approved, send broadcasts, chat with buyers and set auto replies.
For company owners · about 45–60 minutes the first time

Before you start, keep these ready

  • A Facebook account of the owner (used to log in to Meta Business).
  • A phone number for WhatsApp Business API that is not logged in to the normal WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app. It must receive one SMS or call for the code. (If the number is in the app now, delete that WhatsApp account first.)
  • Company details: legal name, address, website, and a document like GST certificate / company registration (for Meta business verification).
  • A debit / credit card for Meta’s message charges. Meta bills you directly; the CRM adds no fee.
  • WhatsApp Marketing must be switched on for your company. If the menu shows a lock, ask the platform admin.
How it works: the CRM uses Meta’s official WhatsApp Cloud API (the same API that AiSensy, Wati and Interakt use). Your number, your Meta account, your bill. Messages go from your number; buyers’ replies come back into the CRM.

Part A — Meta Business account and your number

  1. Open business.facebook.com and log in with Facebook. If you have no business portfolio yet, click Create an account, type your company name, your name and work email.
  2. Go to Settings → Business info and fill legal name, address, phone and website. Then Security Centre → Start verification and upload your GST / registration document. (Verification can take 1–3 days. You can continue the setup meanwhile; until verified, Meta allows about 250 new buyers a day.)
  3. Open developers.facebook.com → My Apps → Create app. Choose use case Other → type Business, give a name such as “Nova Agro CRM”, and pick your business portfolio.
  4. In the new app, find WhatsApp and press Set up. Meta creates a WhatsApp Business Account and a free test number.
  5. In WhatsApp → API Setup, press Add phone number. Fill the display name (your company name, Meta checks it), category and description, then your number. Verify with the SMS or voice code.
  6. Still in API Setup, choose your number in the “From” list. Copy the Phone number ID and the WhatsApp Business Account ID into a notepad.
Common mistake: copying the IDs of the test number. Make sure your own number is selected in the “From” list before you copy the Phone number ID.

Part B — Permanent access token (very important)

The token shown on the API Setup page expires in 24 hours. The CRM needs a permanent token from a “system user”.

  1. In business.facebook.com → Settings → Users → System users → Add. Name it “CRM”, role Admin.
  2. Select the new system user → Assign assets. Add your app (Full control) and your WhatsApp account (Full control).
  3. Press Generate new token. Choose your app, expiry Never, and tick the permissions whatsapp_business_messaging and whatsapp_business_management.
  4. Copy the token (it starts with EAA…). Meta shows it only once — paste it into the CRM straight away.
  5. Also copy two values from developers.facebook.com → your app → App settings → Basic: the App ID and the App secret (press Show).

Part C — Connect in the CRM

  1. In the CRM open WhatsApp Marketing in the left menu. The Setup tab opens by itself.
  2. Paste the Phone number ID, WhatsApp Business Account ID, permanent access token, App ID and App secret.
  3. Press Save & check connection. The CRM asks Meta about the number. You should see Connected · your number · your business name.
The token and app secret are stored encrypted and are never shown again. To change them, paste new ones and save.

Part D — Webhook (delivered / read ticks and buyers’ replies)

  1. In the CRM, Setup tab, copy the Callback URL and the Verify token (click a box to select it).
  2. In developers.facebook.com → your app → WhatsApp → Configuration → Webhook → Edit. Paste both values and press Verify and save.
  3. Below, in Webhook fields, press Subscribe next to messages.
  4. In App settings → Basic, add a Privacy policy URL (your website) and switch the app from Development to Live at the top. In Development mode Meta does not send real updates.
  5. Send “Hi” from your personal WhatsApp to the business number. In the CRM Setup tab the line Last update from WhatsApp shows the time — the webhook works.

Part E — Payment and message prices

  1. In business.facebook.com → WhatsApp Manager → Overview (or Settings) → Payment methods, add your card. Without it, template messages fail.
  2. Meta charges per template message, by the buyer’s country and the type (Marketing or Utility). Replies within 24 hours of the buyer’s message are free.
  3. Open Meta’s rate card (search “WhatsApp pricing rate card”), and type the prices for your main countries in the CRM Message cost box, then Save rates. The CRM uses them only to estimate the cost of each broadcast.

Part F — Templates (needed to start a chat)

WhatsApp lets a business message a buyer first only with a template Meta has approved.

  1. Templates tab → New template.
  2. Name: small letters and _ only, e.g. turmeric_offer. Type: Marketing (offers) or Utility (order / sample updates). Language.
  3. Title: none, text, or a picture (JPG/PNG). Message: use {{1}}, {{2}} for words that change per buyer, and give an example for each.
  4. Optional footer and up to 3 buttons: quick reply (“Send price”), website link, or phone call.
  5. Press Send for approval. Meta usually answers within minutes (sometimes up to 24 hours). Press Sync from WhatsApp to update the status to Approved.
Why templates get rejected: only a variable with no text around it, a missing example, words like “free”/“guaranteed” in a Utility template, or a link shortener. Write a clear business message.

Part G — Send a broadcast

  1. Add a WhatsApp / Zalo number and tags (e.g. turmeric-buyer, dubai) on your leads, or in Bulk Import.
  2. New broadcast tab: give a name, choose an approved template, and for each {{ }} choose what goes in (contact name, company, product, or a fixed text).
  3. Send to: by tags, all leads, or paste numbers with country code. The CRM shows how many numbers and the estimated cost. Buyers who wrote STOP are left out.
  4. Send now or Schedule for a time in the buyers’ day.

In Broadcasts each one shows sent, delivered, read, replied, failed. Numbers lists each buyer and the reason when a message failed. Retry sends again only to the failed numbers. If your daily limit is reached, the rest continue by themselves later.

Part H — Chats and auto replies

  • Chats: every buyer who replies, linked to the lead with the same number. Inside 24 hours of the buyer’s last message you can type freely. After 24 hours, choose a template to restart.
  • Automation: a welcome message for a new chat and keyword replies (e.g. “price, rate” → your price list link). Anyone who writes STOP or UNSUBSCRIBE is opted out of broadcasts automatically.

  • Message only buyers who expect to hear from you. Many “block” or “report” clicks lower your quality rating (shown on the Dashboard) and Meta can limit the number.
  • Keep the same template for similar buyers, and use the buyer’s name and product.
  • Zalo, WeChat, Line and Telegram are separate apps and are not part of WhatsApp broadcasts. Use the WhatsApp / Zalo / Telegram links on each lead for those.

If something does not work

Message in the CRMWhat to do
Invalid OAuth / token expiredYou used the 24-hour token. Make the permanent system-user token (Part B).
Phone number not foundThe Phone number ID is from the test number or a different app. Copy it again with your number selected.
No delivery ticks, no replies in ChatsWebhook not saved, “messages” not subscribed, or the app is still in Development (Part D).
Template not approvedEdit the wording (see Part F) and send again with a new name.
Message failed: payment / account issueAdd or fix the card in WhatsApp Manager (Part E).
Recipient not on the allowed listYou are still sending from the test number. Select your own number.
