Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 22 November 2026

You can ask Travel-Trade CRM to delete your data at any time. This page explains how to remove a connected Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp account, and how to delete your whole Travel-Trade CRM account and its data.

1. Remove a connected Facebook Page or Instagram account

Inside Travel-Trade CRM

  1. Sign in as the company owner.
  2. Open Social Media.
  3. Next to the connected account, click the Remove (bin) button and confirm.

We delete the stored access token for that account straight away. The CRM can then no longer read from or post to it.

From Facebook

  1. On Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (on some versions: Apps and websites).
  2. Find Travel-Trade CRM and click Remove.
  3. To also delete the posts, comments and insights we stored for that account, send us a request as described in section 3.

2. Remove a connected WhatsApp Business number

  1. Sign in as the company owner and open WhatsApp Marketing → Setup.
  2. Click Disconnect number and confirm. We delete the stored access token straight away.
  3. You can also remove Travel-Trade CRM's access to your WhatsApp Business Account in Meta Business Suite → Settings → Apps or System users.
  4. To delete the stored WhatsApp conversations and contact numbers, send us a request as described in section 3.

3. Delete your account and all its data

Email support@traveltrade.co.in with the subject "Data deletion request", sent from the email address registered on the account. Please include:

  • your company name in Travel-Trade CRM;
  • whether you want the whole account deleted, or only data from a connected Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp account;
  • if you want a copy of your data first, say so and we will send you an export before deleting.

Only the company owner can ask to delete a whole company account. Staff members can ask the owner to remove their staff login, or email us to delete their own personal details.

4. What happens next

  • We confirm we have received your request within 48 hours and may ask you to verify that you own the account.
  • We delete the requested data from our live systems within 30 days, and from backups within a further 30 days.
  • We keep only records we must keep by law (for example invoices and tax records), and only for as long as the law requires.
  • We email you when deletion is complete.
What is deleted: the account and staff logins, leads and contacts, follow-ups, tasks, quotations, files, WhatsApp messages and templates, email campaigns, call records and transcripts, social media posts and insights, and all stored access tokens and API keys.

5. If you are a contact of a business that uses Travel-Trade CRM

If a business sent you a message through Travel-Trade CRM and you want your data deleted, contact that business first, because it controls your data. You can also email us at support@traveltrade.co.in with the business name and your phone number or email, and we will pass on your request and help the business complete it.
